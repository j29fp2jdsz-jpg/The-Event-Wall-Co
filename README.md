# The Event Wall Co

Static responsive website for GitHub and Vercel. No build step; output directory `.`.

Nine optimised WebP concept images, accessible image dialogs, mobile navigation and direct enquiry delivery through FormSubmit to the business Gmail inbox. Do not submit test messages without authorization.

## Booking information

Individual walls excluding champagne: one £175, two £275, three £375. Champagne package £275 including plastic flutes and three bottles, subject to confirmed venue and licensing arrangements. Booking fee 20% of quoted total. Walls 6ft wide × 8ft tall plus support footprint. Setup the day before where possible or on event day; collection same day or next day, agreed in advance.

## Operational follow-up

The privacy notice introduces deletion of unsuccessful enquiries within 12 months of last meaningful contact (unless ongoing matters require retention); the business must apply this to its inbox. Confirm legal controller identity/contact details and FormSubmit processing/transfer safeguards; public provider policy does not establish all contractual details. Booking record retention depends on applicable tax/accounting requirements and disputes.

Cancellation/refund terms, remaining balance deadline, delivery charges, controller legal details and alcohol supply model still need business decisions. No non-refundable claim is made. Never represent concept images as completed bookings.
