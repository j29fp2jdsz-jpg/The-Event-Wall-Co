YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who we are

The Event Wall Co is an event wall hire and styling business based in South Wales. We are responsible for the personal information we use to answer enquiries and manage our bookings. For privacy questions or requests, email theeventwallco@gmail.com.

What information we use

We receive information directly from you when you complete our enquiry form or contact us: your name, email address, occasion, event date, venue or town, package and style choices, and anything you include in your message. If you book, we may also need event access details, contact and invoicing information, and records of your quote, booking and payments. Please avoid including sensitive personal information or unnecessary information about guests in your enquiry.

Our website hosting and form providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, request times and security logs when you visit or submit an enquiry.

Why we use it

We use your details to answer questions, prepare quotes, check availability, arrange your event and manage your booking. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract, and performing that contract if you book.

We also have a legitimate interest in keeping our website secure, preventing spam, maintaining relevant correspondence and resolving queries or disputes. We retain information where necessary to meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting requirements. We do not add enquiry contacts to marketing mailing lists automatically.

Who receives your information

Our website is hosted by Vercel. Online enquiries pass through FormSubmit and arrive in our Google Gmail inbox. These services process information needed to host the website, deliver messages and store our correspondence.

For a confirmed event, we may share necessary setup and access information with your venue or a supplier involved in fulfilling your booking. We may also disclose relevant records to professional advisers or authorities where necessary. We do not sell your enquiry details.

Where information is processed

Our service providers operate internationally, so information may be processed outside the UK. Their privacy information is linked above. Contact us if you would like further information about the providers, processing locations or applicable transfer safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence while dealing with your request and any related follow-up. If an enquiry does not become a booking, our retention policy is to delete it within 12 months of the last meaningful contact, unless there is an ongoing complaint or another reason to retain it.

For bookings, we keep the records needed to provide the hire, deal with queries and meet tax, accounting or legal requirements. We review these records and remove information that is no longer needed. Records connected with an active dispute may need to be kept until it is resolved. Service providers may retain separate technical logs or backup copies under their own retention policies.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct your personal information, request deletion or restriction, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or request a portable copy where applicable. These rights depend on the circumstances and we may need to retain some records for legal reasons. If we ever rely on consent for a separate purpose, you can withdraw that consent.

Email theeventwallco@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to check your identity before responding. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

The enquiry form needs a name, email and venue or town so we can respond usefully. Other event details are optional. You can email us directly if you prefer, although our email provider will still process that correspondence.

Cookies and other websites

We have not added advertising pixels, visitor analytics or marketing cookies to this website. Fonts and design images are served with the website. Our hosting and form providers may use technical measures for security and delivery. Links to TikTok and other external services open their own websites, whose privacy and cookie practices apply there.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when the way we handle information changes. The date above shows when it was last updated.
