THE LITTLE DETAILS

A few things
you might be wondering.

Planning something in South Wales? Tell us about your venue, date and ideas so we can work through the details with you.

Can the wall suit my celebration?

That’s the idea. The timber and greenery form the base, with different signs, floral accents and display options for your occasion. Tell us which concept you love and what you’d like to change.

How much space will I need?

Each wall is 6ft wide and 8ft tall (approximately 1.83m × 2.44m). Please allow additional space for the supports, access and photographs. We’ll confirm the full installation footprint and venue suitability before booking.

Can it go inside a marquee?

Marquees are one of the settings we’re designing for. We’ll need details of the floor, available space, weather protection and venue access before confirming the installation.

What are the wall and package prices?

One wall is £175, two walls are £275 and three walls are £375. All individual wall designs qualify except the champagne wall, which is priced separately. The £275 champagne package includes the wall, plastic flutes and 3 bottles of champagne, subject to agreed venue and licensing arrangements. Upgrades and mixed packages are quoted individually.

When do you set up and collect?

We aim to set up the day before your celebration where venue access allows. If that isn’t possible, we can set up on the day. Collection can be the same day or the following day. We’ll agree the dates, access and times with you and your venue before booking.

How do I secure my date?

Once we’ve confirmed availability and your quote, a booking fee of 20% of the quoted total is required to secure your date. Your remaining balance, payment deadline and cancellation or date-change terms will be set out in writing before you pay.

Do you need an electricity supply?

Yes, we need access to electricity for the lights and signs. We have extension cables available where needed. Please tell us about the nearest socket and your venue layout when enquiring.

Is the photo corner an automated photo booth?

It’s a styled timber backdrop and bench for photos using your phone or photographer. Camera equipment, printing and an automated booth aren’t included in the concept shown.

Are these photos from previous bookings?

These are AI-generated concept visualisations of our planned collection, created to help you picture the designs in different settings. Actual build photographs will be added as the collection develops.
