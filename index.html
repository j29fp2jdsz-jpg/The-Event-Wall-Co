THE LITTLE DETAILS

A few things
you might be wondering.

Planning something in South Wales? Tell us about your venue, date and ideas so we can work through the details with you.

Can the wall suit my celebration?

That’s the idea. The timber and greenery form the base, with different signs, floral accents and display options for your occasion. Tell us which concept you love and what you’d like to change.

How much space will I need?

Our planned walls are around 5–6ft wide and 8ft tall. Allow additional space for supports, access and photos. We’ll confirm the final footprint and venue suitability before a booking is agreed.

Can it go inside a marquee?

Marquees are one of the settings we’re designing for. We’ll need details of the floor, available space, weather protection and venue access before confirming the installation.

What’s included, and how much is hire?

Pricing and inclusions will be confirmed for your chosen setup. Glassware, drinks, signs, flowers, delivery and collection should all be agreed in your quote. The images show styling ideas rather than a fixed package.

Is the photo corner an automated photo booth?

It’s a styled timber backdrop and bench for photos using your phone or photographer. Camera equipment, printing and an automated booth aren’t included in the concept shown.

Are these photos from previous bookings?

These are AI-generated concept visualisations of our planned collection, created to help you picture the designs in different settings. Actual build photographs will be added as the collection develops.
